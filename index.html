    Data and method

    Boundaries are the IEBC county, constituency and ward polygons (60 wards in Kiambu), simplified for fast loading. Areas are computed from these polygons. Kahawa Sukari ward (Ruiru) had no polygon in the source, so it is approximated as the part of Ruiru not covered by the other wards.

    Kabete ward populations come from the 2009 census. The 2019 figures per ward are estimates, scaling each ward by Kabete's overall growth (140,427 to 199,653). Replace them when KNBS ward tables are available.
